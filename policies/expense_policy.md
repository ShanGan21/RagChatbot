# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting Expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted later than 60 days will not be reimbursed. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.

## Approval Limits
Managers may approve expenses up to $1,000. Expenses between $1,000 and $5,000 require director approval. Expenses above $5,000 require VP approval and must be pre-approved.

## Air and Hotel Travel
Employees must book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights over 6 hours with director approval. Hotel stays are capped at $250 per night in major cities and $175 per night elsewhere. Bookings must be made through the company travel portal.

## Meals
Daily meal allowance during business travel is $75. Alcohol is not reimbursable except during approved client entertainment events.

## Reimbursement Timing
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit.
