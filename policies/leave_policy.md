# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per year, accrued monthly. Employees with 5 or more years of service accrue 25 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata based on scheduled hours. PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

## PTO Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on December 31. Unused PTO is paid out on termination only where required by local law.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry over.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption and may be taken in one block or two blocks.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 11 paid public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published each January by HR.
