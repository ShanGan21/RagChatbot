# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Roles marked as hybrid-eligible may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a VP and HR. New hires must work on-site for their first 30 days.

## Core Hours
Regardless of location, employees must be available between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their team's primary time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible.

## Home Office Stipend
Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time $500 home office stipend, and a $50 monthly internet and phone allowance. Receipts must be submitted through the expense system within 60 days of purchase.

## Working from Abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year with manager approval. Longer stays require approval from HR and Legal due to tax and immigration implications. Employees may never work from sanctioned countries.
