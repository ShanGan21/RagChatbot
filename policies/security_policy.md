# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and Authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company systems. Passwords must be stored in the company-approved password manager and never shared.

## Devices
Only company-managed devices may access production systems. All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour.

## Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts, and must be encrypted when sent outside the company.

## Reporting Incidents
Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails, must be reported to security@company.example immediately. Employees will not be penalized for good-faith reports.

## Software Installation
Employees may not install unapproved software on company devices. Requests for new software go through the IT service desk and are reviewed within 5 business days.
